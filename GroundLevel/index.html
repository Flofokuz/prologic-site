The path

You start on the street, not in an office. The first days show us both whether the freedom and the structure of this work fit you.

  1. Apply. Send your resume below, or just tell us about yourself if you don't have one yet.
  2. Shadow day with Prince. Work next to me in my area, or pick a place you think you'd want to work. You choose your location from day one. I pull up, we look at that area together, and I show you what a free, untethered street sales entrepreneur looks for.
  3. Wrap-up and core trajectory session. We talk through what you saw and map out where you want this to take you.
  4. Side by side. One to two weeks of daily sales next to a teammate while you onboard.
  5. Office meeting in Times Square. Once you've shown you can handle the street, we bring you into the office side.
