Pathfinders

A program led by Marcus, our Xtra Hype Connection co-host, for people with a big idea who feel the pull to lead the way.

Now forming

"You didn't find yourself on a path. You found yourself on a wheel." Pathfinders is about cutting your way off that wheel and making a path other people can follow.

  • 12-week cohorts, run as workshops about three weeks at a time, with real tasks and community outreach.
  • No bosses, no owners. Everyone who comes through helps build it. You learn to do by doing.
  • It takes desire and effort. Nobody is forced, and it's not a freebie.
  • Stewards, the people who help run it, come in through an interview and application.
  • The curriculum, lesson plans and structure are being built now, and it pairs with Prologic's own leadership curriculum.

Five questions we start with

Run them on a job, a relationship, a business or a whole block. Then go around again.

  • EarthWhat are you standing on? What's actually solid?
  • WaterWhy? What is this for?
  • AirWho's looking at this with you? Get outside eyes.
  • FireWhen? What's the fuel, and what do you need to cut?
  • VoidWho are you becoming, and what do you need to let go of?
