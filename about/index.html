The Dungeon Master

Life is the playing board, and I'm the dungeon master.

Most of us are already halfway through the maze before anybody hands us a light. Then you strike a torch, and the flash shows you what's been around you the whole time: the pitfalls, the traps, the pressure closing in. That moment is scary. It's also the first moment you can do something about it.

That's what my tools are made for. They help you navigate the darkness, trace the pitfalls, see the traps coming and survive the pressures of the world we find ourselves in. With the tools, your gear and your own talents, we take on the trials we set ourselves up to conquer.

Maybe we came here to do a job or finish a task, maybe not. Either way, the work looks like this, and you go around as many times as it takes:

  • EarthSift through the dust, dirt and ashes of your life. Find the sediment solid enough to build a foundation on.
  • WaterCleanse it and mold it into a shape that serves you.
  • AirLet it dry and harden. Give it time and outside eyes.
  • FirePut it through the furnace so the new form holds under pressure.
  • VoidCarry what you became into the next round. Then go around again.

Every cycle, you find the function. Then you give it form. Form follows function.
