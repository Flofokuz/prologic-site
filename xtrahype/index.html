House rules

Marcus runs through these at the top of every show. They keep the room respectful and easy to hear.

  1. Stay muted until you speak. A TV or a conversation behind you goes out to everybody.
  2. Wait for the floor. Speak when someone yields or passes the mic. When you're done, say “I yield” so the next person knows.
  3. Stay on topic. If it's a different subject, hold it for the open floor.
  4. Let the hosts handle interruptions. If something goes sideways, Marcus and Flo will sort it out.
